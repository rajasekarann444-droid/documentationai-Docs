## Overview

The Risk Register is the workspace for accessing risk records within the Risk Management module. Each record shows inherent scores, residual scores, category, and overdue status so you can identify critical risks and overdue items at a glance.

See [Risk Management Overview](/risk-management-overview) for summary metrics and analysis panels. Browse [Risk Library](/risk-library) for predefined risk scenarios to add to your register.

## Accessing the Risk Register

1. Open **Risk Management** in Stakflo.
2. Select the **Risk Register** tab.
3. Review the available risk records.

## Risk Assessment Context

The Risk Management overview summarizes risk information using metrics such as inherent scores, residual scores, overdue risks, and critical risks.

Use the Risk Register to access risk records for further review.
