## Overview

The Risk Management dashboard provides an overview of risk information and helps you review the current risk posture.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Stakflo advertises **Risk Awareness** and a **Risk-to-Remediation Engine** as platform capabilities — described as surfacing emerging compliance gaps early and providing AI-driven insights to detect compliance drift and evolving risks. These are advertised capabilities that may not yet be fully documented. See the [Introduction](/stackflo-introduction) page for the full list of advertised capabilities.
</Callout>

## Risk Summary

The Overview tab displays these metrics:

- **Open Risks:** Risks that remain open.
- **Critical Risks:** Risks with an inherent score greater than 20.
- **Overdue Risks:** Open risks that have passed their due date.
- **Assessed Risks:** Risks with an inherent score.
- **Residual Scored:** Risks with a residual score.

## Risk Analysis

The dashboard includes panels for:

- **Risk by Category:** View the distribution of risks by category.
- **Inherent Score Bands:** Review risks grouped by inherent score.
- **Treatment Mix:** Review risk treatment information.
- **Inherent Heat Map:** View the inherent or residual risk heat map.
- **Inherent Risk Posture:** Review the inherent or residual risk posture.

Some panels may display an empty state when no data is available.

## Risk Management Tabs

The module includes three tabs:

- **Overview:** Review risk metrics and analysis panels.
- **Risk Register:** Access the risk register. See [Risk Register](/risk-register) for details on reviewing individual risk records.
- **Risk Library:** Access the risk library. See [Risk Library](/risk-library) for predefined risk scenarios you can add to your register.
