## Set up your Stakflo workspace

This guide walks through the essential setup steps to get your Stakflo workspace ready for compliance management.

## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

- You have a Stakflo account. If you do not have one, contact your administrator or email support@stakflo.com.
- You have admin or workspace owner permissions.
- You have credentials for the integrations you plan to connect, such as AWS, Okta, or GitHub.
- For login instructions, see [Introduction](/introduction).

</Callout>

## Step 1: Set up your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Stakflo" icon="log-in" title-type="p">

Navigate to the platform and sign in with your credentials or SSO. See [Introduction](/introduction).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the setup wizard" icon="zap" title-type="p">

Follow the onboarding wizard to configure your workspace name, team members, and organization details. See [Quickstart](/quickstart).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete onboarding tasks" icon="check-square" title-type="p">

Finish any remaining onboarding checklist items from the dashboard. See [Concepts](/concepts).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review your dashboard" icon="layout-dashboard" title-type="p">

Familiarize yourself with the Stakflo dashboard layout and widgets. See [Dashboard overviews](/dashboard-overviews).

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Your workspace is ready when the onboarding checklist shows all tasks complete and your dashboard displays compliance status widgets.

</Callout>

## Step 2: Connect integrations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Integrations" icon="plug" title-type="p">

Go to the Integrations page from the sidebar. See [Integrations overview](/integrations/overview).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select an integration" icon="list" title-type="p">

Choose from available integrations such as AWS, GCP, Azure, Okta, or Google Workspace.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Authenticate" icon="key" title-type="p">

Enter the required credentials, such as an API key, OAuth token, or service account, and authorize the connection.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the connection" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

Confirm the integration shows as Connected and that data begins syncing.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Repeat for additional integrations" icon="repeat" title-type="p">

Connect all relevant data sources for comprehensive evidence collection.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Integrations are successfully connected when each shows a green Connected status on the Integrations page.

</Callout>

## Step 3: Select compliance frameworks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Frameworks" icon="shield" title-type="p">

Go to the Frameworks page from the sidebar. See [Frameworks](/compliance/frameworks).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a framework" icon="list" title-type="p">

Select from supported frameworks such as SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, or HIPAA.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review framework requirements" icon="eye" title-type="p">

Examine the controls and requirements associated with your selected framework.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Map unified controls" icon="git-branch" title-type="p">

Use the Unified Controls feature to map overlapping requirements across multiple frameworks. See [Unified Controls](/unified-controls).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure policies" icon="file-text" title-type="p">

Set up or import the policies required by your selected framework. See [Policies](/compliance/policies).

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Your framework is active when its controls appear on your dashboard with completion status indicators.

</Callout>

## Setup verification checklist

<Callout kind="info">

Confirm the following before moving on to ongoing compliance management:

- Onboarding checklist shows all tasks complete
- At least one integration connected and syncing
- At least one compliance framework enabled
- Policies imported or created for your selected framework
- Dashboard displays compliance status widgets

</Callout>
