## Getting started

Welcome to Stakflo. Find answers about logging in, setting up your workspace, and using the dashboard.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What is Stakflo?" default-open="false">
    Stakflo is a compliance and risk management platform that helps organizations automate evidence collection, manage policies, track risks, and prepare for audits across multiple frameworks.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I log in to Stakflo?" default-open="false">
    Navigate to the Stakflo login page. Sign in with your email and password, or use SSO if your organization has configured it. See the [login guide](/introduction) for details.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Do I need to install anything?" default-open="false">
    No. Stakflo is a cloud-based SaaS platform accessible through your web browser, so there is nothing to install or deploy.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I access the Stakflo dashboard?" default-open="false">
    After you log in, Stakflo takes you to the dashboard by default. The dashboard provides an overview of your compliance posture, tasks, and recent activity. See the [dashboard walkthrough](/dashboard-overviews).
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Frameworks and compliance

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Which compliance frameworks does Stakflo support?" default-open="false">
    Stakflo supports SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS, NIST CSF 2.0, and CIS Controls v8. See [Frameworks](/compliance/frameworks) for the full list.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I select a compliance framework?" default-open="false">
    Navigate to **Frameworks > Manage Frameworks** and enable the frameworks your organization needs. See [Frameworks](/compliance/frameworks) for step-by-step guidance.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I use multiple frameworks at once?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Stakflo's unified control framework maps controls across multiple standards, so you can implement controls once and satisfy requirements across frameworks. See [Unified Controls](/unified-controls).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I manage policies?" default-open="false">
    Create policies from templates or from scratch, import existing documents, and manage the approval workflow. See [Policies](/compliance/policies) for details.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Integrations

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What integrations are available?" default-open="false">
    Stakflo integrates with cloud providers such as AWS, GCP, and Azure; identity providers such as Okta, Google Workspace, and Entra ID; MDM tools, HRIS systems, ticketing systems, and monitoring tools. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I connect an integration?" default-open="false">
    Navigate to **Integrations > Add Integration**, select the tool, authenticate, and configure the evidence collection scope. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why is my integration not syncing?" default-open="false">
    Common causes include expired credentials or insufficient permissions. See the [integration troubleshooting guide](/help-center/troubleshooting/install-error).
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Advertised capabilities

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Does Stakflo use AI for compliance tasks?" default-open="false">
    Stakflo advertises several AI-driven capabilities including a **Policy Intelligence Engine**, **Risk-to-Remediation Engine**, and **Vendor Risk Intelligence**. These are advertised capabilities; availability may vary by plan. See the [Introduction](/stackflo-introduction) page for the full list.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Is Trust Center Automation available?" default-open="false">
    Stakflo advertises **Trust Center Automation** as a platform capability for publishing a continuously updated Trust Center. This is an advertised capability that may not yet be fully documented. Contact [support@stakflo.com](mailto:support@stakflo.com) to confirm availability for your plan.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I generate board-level compliance reports?" default-open="false">
    Stakflo advertises **Board Reports** as a capability for generating executive-ready compliance and risk reports. This is an advertised capability. Contact [support@stakflo.com](mailto:support@stakflo.com) to confirm availability.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
