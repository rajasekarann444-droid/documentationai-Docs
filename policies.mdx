## Overview

The Policies module provides an overview of policy records and their current status.

## Policy Summary

The Overview tab displays these metrics:

- **Total Policies:** Total number of policies.
- **Published:** Policies that have been published.
- **In Review:** Policies currently under review.
- **Draft:** Policies that remain in draft.
- **Acceptance Rate:** The displayed policy acceptance rate.

## Policy Insights

The Overview includes charts and panels for:

- **Policy Status:** Distribution of policies by status.
- **Approval Workflow:** Approved and pending policy counts.
- **Acceptance Rate:** Policy acceptance information.
- **Framework Coverage:** Frameworks associated with policies.
- **Workforce Assignment:** Workforce assignment information.
- **Expiry / Review Due:** Expiry and review information when available.

## Policy Tabs

The Policies module includes:

- **Overview:** Review policy summary metrics and charts.
- **All Policies:** Browse policy records.
- **My Approvals:** Review policies assigned for approval.

Use the relevant tab to find the policy information you need.
