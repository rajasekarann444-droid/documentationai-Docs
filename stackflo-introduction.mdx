Stakflo is an AI-native compliance and risk management platform that helps organizations maintain continuous audit readiness, improve compliance visibility, identify risks, and centralize compliance operations across frameworks, systems, and teams.

Use this documentation to understand Stakflo's modules and learn how to navigate the application.

## What is Stakflo?

Stakflo unifies compliance operations, controls, evidence, audits, policies, and vendor risk into a single platform with structured, automated workflows. The result is reduced manual overhead and a continuously audit-ready posture.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false" color="#10b981">
  **Advertised capabilities**

  - **Always Audit-Ready**: Always-on monitoring provides real-time insight into control coverage, compliance posture, and audit readiness across frameworks.
  - **Prioritized Controls**: AI highlights controls and gaps that carry the highest compliance and security risk, so teams address what matters first.
  - **Unified Audits**: Manage audits, evidence, and attestations through a single, structured workflow, eliminating manual coordination and audit fatigue.
  - **Vendor Risk Intelligence**: AI analyzes third-party artifacts such as SOC 2 reports, Trust Center data, ISO evidence, questionnaires, and attestations, producing auditable vendor risk insights without manual review.
  - **Policy Intelligence Engine**: AI parses legacy policies, extracts control intent, and automatically maps requirements across compliance frameworks, reducing policy rewrite effort.
  - **Trust Center Automation**: Validate security and compliance claims against real evidence and publish a continuously updated Trust Center, building customer trust without manual effort.
  - **Risk-to-Remediation Engine**: AI-driven insights detect compliance drift and evolving risks, enabling organizations to proactively remediate and maintain control.
  - **Compliance Visibility**: Track framework coverage, control effectiveness, and audit status in real time, with clear ownership and accountability.
  - **Risk Awareness**: Surface emerging compliance gaps and high-risk controls early, before audits or customer reviews expose them.
  - **Centralized Operations**: Coordinate audits, evidence collection, policy updates, and reviews across teams from a single system of record.
  - **Multi-Framework Coverage**: Monitor progress across multiple standards, including SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR, with unified mapping and shared controls.
  - **Audit-Ready Evidence**: Maintain continuously updated, audit-ready evidence, reducing scramble, rework, and last-minute documentation.
  - **Board Reports**: Generate clear, executive-ready compliance and risk reports that support leadership decisions and customer assurance.

  These capabilities are advertised on the [official Stakflo product page](https://sq1.security/products/stakflo). This documentation covers features and workflows verified in the application where noted. Not all advertised capabilities may be fully documented yet.
</Callout>
