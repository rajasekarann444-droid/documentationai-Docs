Stakflo helps organizations manage compliance, monitor risks, manage policies and evidence, and assess third-party vendors in one platform.

Use this documentation to understand Stakflo's modules and learn how to navigate the application.

## Explore Stakflo

### Compliance Management

Manage compliance frameworks, unified controls, policies, and evidence.

### Risk Management

Review risk information, track risks, and understand risk assessments.

### Third-Party Risk Management

Maintain third-party vendor information and review vendor risk and assessment status.

### Dashboard

Get an overview of compliance progress, framework status, policy status, and recent tasks.

## Getting Started

Begin with the Dashboard Overview to understand the main workspace. Then explore each module to learn about its available features and actions.
