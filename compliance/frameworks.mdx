## Frameworks and controls

<Callout kind="info" title="Who can use this feature">

Framework management is available to Admin and Manager roles. Supported plans include Foundation with up to 2 frameworks, Growth with up to 5 frameworks, and Scale with unlimited frameworks.

</Callout>

## Unified control framework

The unified control framework is a single control set that maps across multiple frameworks. Implement a control once, then use the mapped implementation to satisfy requirements across several standards.

For example, when you implement an **Access Control Policy**, ComplyHub can map it to SOC 2 CC6.1, ISO 27001 A.9.1.1, HIPAA §164.312(a)(1), and corresponding requirements in other enabled frameworks.

This approach helps you:

- Reduce audit preparation time.
- Eliminate duplicate control work.
- Maintain consistent implementation details across frameworks.
- Collect and reuse evidence for related requirements.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Pro Tip!">

Map controls to your most demanding framework first, typically SOC 2 or ISO 27001. The unified framework automatically populates mappings for less stringent frameworks, saving hours of manual work.

</Callout>

## Selecting frameworks

Use framework management to enable the standards your organization needs to track.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open framework management" icon="settings">

Navigate to `Frameworks > Manage Frameworks`.

</Step>

<Step title="Browse the library" icon="search">

Review the available frameworks and their control counts.

</Step>

<Step title="Enable a framework" icon="plus">

Select **Enable** on each framework you need.

</Step>

<Step title="Review auto-mapped controls" icon="check-circle">

Open the unified view to see how the new controls map to existing controls.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" title="Note:">

Enabling a framework immediately adds its controls to your task list. Confirm that your team has capacity to manage the additional requirements before enabling multiple frameworks at the same time.

</Callout>

## Mapping controls

Review and adjust framework mappings from the unified control list.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open a control" icon="folder-open">

Select a control from the unified control list.

</Step>

<Step title="Review auto-mappings" icon="eye">

Check which framework requirements ComplyHub mapped automatically.

</Step>

<Step title="Adjust mappings" icon="edit">

Add or modify manual mappings when the automatic mappings do not match your implementation.

</Step>

<Step title="Save" icon="save">

Save the control with its updated mappings.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Managing controls

### Viewing controls

Filter controls by framework, status, owner, or risk level to find the requirements that need attention.

### Editing controls

Update implementation details, evidence requirements, and risk ratings as your compliance program changes.

### Assigning owners

Link controls to department owners so each requirement has a clear person or team responsible for implementation and evidence.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Can I add a custom framework?">

Yes. Use the Custom Framework Builder, available on Growth and Scale plans.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How does control mapping work between SOC 2 and ISO 27001?">

The unified framework automatically maps common controls between SOC 2 and ISO 27001. Review the mappings and adjust them manually when your implementation requires a different relationship.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if a control does not apply to my organization?">

Mark the control as **N/A** and provide a justification. ComplyHub excludes controls marked N/A from your readiness score.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I export my control mappings?">

Yes. Navigate to `Reports > Control Mapping Export` to download your mappings as a CSV or PDF.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
