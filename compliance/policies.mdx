## Manage your compliance policies

Policies are the foundation of your compliance program. Stakflo lets you create policies from templates, import existing documents, and manage the full lifecycle from draft to publication to revision.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Stakflo advertises a **Policy Intelligence Engine** capability for intelligent policy management and cross-framework mapping. Availability may vary by plan. See the full list of advertised capabilities on the [Introduction](/stackflo-introduction) page.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Policy management is available to Admin and Manager roles. Policy templates are available on all plans. Custom policy creation is available on Growth and Scale plans.
</Callout>

## Creating a policy

Create a policy from a template or write one from scratch, then submit it for approval before publication.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new policy" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Navigate to `Policies > Create New`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a starting point" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Select a template or create the policy from scratch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter details" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Add the policy title and category.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write content" icon="pen-tool" title-type="p">
    Use the Markdown editor to write or edit the policy body.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Insert variables" icon="code" title-type="p">
    Add dynamic variables for content that Stakflo should populate automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign approvers" icon="users" title-type="p">
    Select the people who must approve the policy before publication.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit for review" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Click **Submit** to start the approval workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Using templates

The template library includes pre-written policies for common compliance requirements:

- Acceptable Use Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Incident Response Policy
- Data Retention Policy
- Password Policy
- Remote Work Policy

Templates align with SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR requirements. After you create a policy from a template, you can edit the content to match your organization.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Start with a template even if you already have an existing policy. Templates include framework-aligned control mappings that automatically link your policy to the appropriate controls.
</Callout>

### Adding variables

Variables let you reuse organization-specific information without updating each policy manually. Stakflo populates supported variables from your organization settings.

Supported variables include:

- `{{company_name}}`
- `{{effective_date}}`
- `{{review_date}}`
- `{{owner_name}}`
- `{{department}}`

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Define variables in **Organization Settings** before using them in a policy. Undefined variables appear as blank text in published policies.
</Callout>

## Importing policies

Import an existing policy when you want to preserve source content instead of starting with a template or blank document.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open import" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Navigate to `Policies > Import`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload document" icon="file-up" title-type="p">
    Upload your policy file in `.docx`, `.pdf`, or `.md` format.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review content" icon="eye" title-type="p">
    Check the imported content in the editor and correct any formatting or content changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map to controls" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Link policy sections to the relevant control requirements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save as draft" icon="save" title-type="p">
    Save the imported policy as a draft for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Imported PDFs may lose formatting. For the best results, import a `.docx` or `.md` file. Always review imported content before publishing.
</Callout>

## Publishing policies

Publish a policy after its content has been reviewed and all required approvers have approved it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the draft" icon="folder-open" title-type="p">
    Open the policy you want to publish.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit for approval" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Click **Submit for Approval** to notify the assigned approvers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approver reviews" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Approvers receive a notification and review the policy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish" icon="globe" title-type="p">
    After approval, click **Publish** to make the policy live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Policy goes live" icon="check" title-type="p">
    The policy becomes available on your policy portal and is added to the employee onboarding checklist.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Published policies cannot be edited directly. Create a new revision to make changes.
</Callout>

## Revision history

Every published change creates a new revision. To view the history, go to `Policies > Policy Name > Revision History`.

Revision History lets you:

- Compare revisions side by side.
- Review when each revision was created.
- Restore a previous revision when needed.
- Track how policy content changed over time.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use revision history to demonstrate policy evolution during audits. Auditors want to see that policies are reviewed and updated regularly, not only stored as static documents.
</Callout>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I require employees to acknowledge policies?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Enable policy acknowledgments in **Settings**. Employees receive a task to read and acknowledge each published policy.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How often should policies be reviewed?" default-open="false">
    Stakflo recommends annual reviews. Set review reminders in the policy settings to receive a notification before the review date.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I customize a template?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Templates are fully editable after you create a policy from them. Your changes apply only to the new policy and do not modify the original template.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I share policies with external auditors?" default-open="false">
    Use the Trust Portal or export the policy to PDF from the policy page. You can also grant auditors read-only access during an audit window.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
