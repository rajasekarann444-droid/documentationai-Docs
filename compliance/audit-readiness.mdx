## Audit readiness

Audit readiness measures how prepared your organization is to pass an external compliance audit. Stakflo automates evidence collection, tracks your readiness score, and centralizes audit management so you can approach audit day with confidence.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This workflow reflects Stakflo's **Always Audit-Ready** and **Unified Audits** capabilities — continuous audit readiness across frameworks with a unified evidence and control set. See the full list of advertised capabilities on the [Introduction](/stackflo-introduction) page.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Audit readiness is available to Admin and Manager roles on all plans. External auditor access is available on Growth and Scale plans.
</Callout>

## Audit Center Overview

The Audit Center is the central hub for managing internal and external audits. Create audit tracks for each framework, assign internal auditors, invite external auditors, and track audit progress, findings, and remediation from one place.

## Preparing for audits

Use the following workflow to identify and resolve gaps before you schedule an external audit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your readiness score" icon="gauge" title-type="p">
    Review the readiness score on the dashboard to see your overall compliance posture.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clear open tasks" icon="check-square" title-type="p">
    Address all open and overdue tasks in the Task Center.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify evidence" icon="folder-check" title-type="p">
    Confirm that all required evidence is collected and current.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review control details" icon="clipboard" title-type="p">
    Check that implementation details for all in-scope controls are complete.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run a readiness report" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Generate a readiness report to identify any remaining gaps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Do not schedule an external audit until your readiness score is above 85%. Auditors will request evidence for all in-scope controls, and gaps discovered during the audit can result in findings or certification delays.
</Callout>

## Evidence collection

Stakflo supports both automated and manual evidence collection. Use automated evidence wherever an integration can provide the required data, and upload evidence manually for controls that need supporting documents or files.

### Automated evidence

Stakflo collects evidence from connected integrations on a scheduled basis. Examples include AWS CloudTrail logs, Okta user access reports, and Intune device compliance reports.

No manual action is needed after you connect and configure the integrations. Stakflo adds newly collected evidence to the relevant controls according to each integration's collection schedule.

### Manual evidence

Upload evidence manually for controls without automated collection. Stakflo supports PDF files, screenshots, CSV files, XLSX files, and logs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the evidence task" icon="folder-open" title-type="p">
    Find the evidence request in the Task Center.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload evidence" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Select **Upload Evidence**, then choose one or more files.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add notes" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Describe what the evidence demonstrates and identify the period it covers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit for review" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Submit the evidence for manager review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Tag manual evidence with the control ID and evidence period. This makes it easier for auditors to locate and verify evidence during the audit.
</Callout>

## Readiness reports

Generate a readiness report from **Audit Readiness > Reports > Generate Readiness Report**. Select the framework and date range, then generate the report.

Each report includes:

- Overall readiness score
- Control-by-control status
- Evidence coverage
- Gaps and recommendations

Export the report as a PDF to share it with stakeholders or auditors.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Readiness reports reflect real-time data. Generate a fresh report before sharing it with auditors or stakeholders.
</Callout>

## Scheduling external audits

Schedule an external audit after you have reviewed your readiness score, resolved open tasks, and verified evidence for every in-scope control.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open external audits" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Navigate to **Audit Readiness > External Audits**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule the audit" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Select **Schedule External Audit**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select scope" icon="target" title-type="p">
    Choose the framework and define the audit scope.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter auditor details" icon="user-plus" title-type="p">
    Invite the external auditor to the platform.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the audit window" icon="clock" title-type="p">
    Define the audit start and end dates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant access" icon="key" title-type="p">
    Give the auditor read-only access to the controls and evidence included in the audit scope.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  External auditor access is limited to the audit window. Stakflo automatically revokes access after the end date unless an Admin extends the audit window.
</Callout>

## Managing audit findings

Auditors submit findings through the Audit Center. Each finding links to the relevant control, so you can review the affected requirement and supporting evidence.

Create remediation tasks directly from findings, assign owners, set due dates, and track progress to closure. Stakflo updates the finding status automatically as the remediation task progresses.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How often should I run a readiness report?" default-open="false">
    Run a readiness report at least monthly. During active audit preparation, run one weekly to track progress closely and identify new gaps.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens if evidence expires before the audit?" default-open="false">
    Expired evidence appears as a gap in the readiness report. Upload fresh evidence before the audit window opens.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can auditors see all our data?" default-open="false">
    No. Auditors only see the controls and evidence within the audit scope you define when scheduling the audit.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I handle audit findings?" default-open="false">
    Create a remediation task from the finding, assign an owner, set a due date, and track progress to closure. The finding status updates automatically as the remediation task progresses.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
