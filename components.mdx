## Getting around Stakflo

Learn how to navigate Stakflo's dashboard, find your tasks, and stay on top of compliance activity. Understanding the layout helps you move quickly between frameworks, policies, and evidence.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  All roles can access the dashboard and task center. Some sections may be restricted based on role permissions.
</Callout>

## Dashboard overview

The dashboard is your compliance command center. It provides a real-time view of your organization's compliance posture and highlights what needs attention.

- **Readiness Score**: Overall compliance posture with a breakdown by framework
- **Task Summary**: Open tasks, overdue items, and completion rate
- **Recent Activity**: Latest evidence uploads, policy changes, and audit updates
- **Upcoming Audits**: Scheduled internal and external audits

The dashboard is customizable. Drag and drop widgets to arrange them in the order that matters most to you.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Stakflo brings audits, evidence, policies, and reviews into a single system, the **Centralized Operations** capability. See the [Introduction](/stackflo-introduction) page for the full list of advertised capabilities.
</Callout>

## Task Center

The Task Center is the central hub for all compliance tasks, including evidence requests, policy reviews, control testing, and remediation. It's where your team goes to find what needs to be done.

### Task types

- **Evidence Collection**: Upload or verify evidence for specific controls
- **Policy Review**: Review and approve draft policies
- **Control Testing**: Validate that implemented controls are operating effectively
- **Remediation**: Address audit findings or identified gaps

### Completing a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the task" icon="folder-open" title-type="p">
    Click on a task from the Task Center to view its details and requirements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review requirements" icon="eye" title-type="p">
    Read the task description, linked control, and evidence requirements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take action" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Upload evidence, review a policy, or complete the required action.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit for review" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Submit the completed task for manager or admin review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use the My Tasks filter to focus on items assigned to you. Set up daily digest emails in `Settings > Notifications` to receive a summary of overdue and upcoming tasks each morning.
</Callout>

## Notification Center

The notification center keeps you informed about compliance activity. Click the bell icon in the top-right corner to view recent notifications.

### Notification types

- Task assignments and due date reminders
- Evidence overdue alerts
- Policy approval requests
- Audit reminders and scheduling updates
- Integration health alerts, including connection failures and token expiration

Configure email digest frequency in `Settings > Notifications`. Choose from real-time, daily digest, or weekly summary.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Critical notifications, such as overdue evidence and failed integrations, are always sent via email in real time, regardless of your digest settings.
</Callout>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I customize my dashboard layout?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Drag and drop widgets to rearrange them. Your layout is saved per user, so each team member can customize their own view.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I filter tasks by framework?" default-open="false">
    Use the framework filter dropdown at the top of the Task Center to narrow tasks to a specific compliance framework.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I dismiss notifications?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Click the X on individual notifications to dismiss them. Critical alerts cannot be dismissed until the underlying issue is resolved.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Where do I find audit reports?" default-open="false">
    Navigate to `Reports > Audit Reports`. You can filter by framework, date range, and audit type.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
