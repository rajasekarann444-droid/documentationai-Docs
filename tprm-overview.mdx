## Overview

The Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) dashboard provides an overview of third-party vendors, their risk levels, and assessment progress.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Stakflo advertises **Vendor Risk Intelligence** as a platform capability — described as AI-driven analysis of third-party artifacts such as SOC 2 reports, Trust Center data, ISO evidence, and questionnaires to produce auditable vendor risk insights. This is an advertised capability that may not yet be fully documented. See the [Introduction](/stackflo-introduction) page for the full list of advertised capabilities.
</Callout>

## Risk Summary

The dashboard includes these metrics:

- **Total TPRM:** Total number of third-party vendors.
- **High Risk TPRM:** Vendors classified as high risk.
- **Medium Risk TPRM:** Vendors classified as medium risk.
- **Low Risk TPRM:** Vendors classified as low risk.
- **Assessment Completion:** Overview of assessment completion.
- **Pending Assessments:** Assessments awaiting completion.
- **High Risk Share:** Proportion of vendors classified as high risk.

## Dashboard Charts

The Overview page includes two charts:

- **Third-Party Risk Management By Risk Level:** Shows the distribution of vendors by risk level.
- **Assessment Status:** Shows the status of vendor assessments.

## Inventory

Use the **Inventory** tab to access the third-party vendor list and review individual vendor information. See [Vendor Inventory](/inventory) for the full vendor list and filtering options.
