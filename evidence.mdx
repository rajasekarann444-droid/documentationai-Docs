## Overview

The Evidence module provides an overview of evidence sources and their current status.

## Evidence Summary

The Overview tab displays four summary metrics:

- **OK:** Evidence sources marked as OK.
- **Needs remediation:** Evidence sources requiring remediation.
- **Integration:** Evidence sources associated with integrations.
- **Custom:** Custom evidence sources.

## Evidence Categories

The overview includes a category breakdown showing how evidence sources are distributed across areas such as:

- Source Control & Version Control
- Endpoint Security & EDR
- Identity & Access Management
- Work Management & Collaboration
- CI/CD & DevOps Automation
- HR & People Systems
- Cloud Infrastructure & Platforms
- Cloud Security & Compliance
- IT Service Management
- Custom evidence

The category chart helps you understand the distribution of evidence sources across your organization.

## All Evidence

Use the **All Evidence** tab to access the evidence list.

Review the available evidence information and identify sources that may require remediation.
