Congrats on signing up for Stakflo! Learn how to access your account for the first time and for returning visits, so you can start your compliance journey right away.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  First-time users receive an email invitation link and must activate their account before logging in. If your organization has SSO enforced, you will authenticate through your identity provider instead of a password.
</Callout>

## For First-Time Users

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your email" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Look for an email from Stakflo with your unique access link. Check spam if you don't see it within 5 minutes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the activation link" icon="mouse-pointer-click" title-type="p">
    Click the Login button in the email to activate your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your credentials" icon="key-round" title-type="p">
    Create a password or authenticate via SSO if your admin has configured it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your profile" icon="user-check" title-type="p">
    Add your name and role, then access the dashboard to begin onboarding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## For Returning Users

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page" icon="globe" title-type="p">
    Navigate to your Stakflo login page at `app.stakflo.com`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Use your registered work email address and click Continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in" icon="log-in" title-type="p">
    Authenticate via SSO with Okta, Google, or Microsoft Entra, or request a one-time verification code sent to your email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## SSO Login

Stakflo supports single sign-on through Okta, Google Workspace, and Microsoft Entra ID. Admins can configure SSO from `Settings > SSO Configuration`. Once enabled, users authenticate through their identity provider instead of a separate password. For setup details, see [Account, Access & Support](/authentication).

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If your organization has SSO enforced, you will not see the one-time password option on the login screen. Contact your admin if you need password-based access.
</Callout>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I change my password" default-open="false">
    Navigate to `Profile > Security > Reset Password`. If SSO is enforced, password management is handled by your identity provider.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="I didn't receive the invitation email. What should I do?" default-open="false">
    Check your spam folder first. If it's not there, ask your admin to resend the invitation from `Settings > Team Management`.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I enable SSO for my team?" default-open="false">
    See the [Account, Access & Support](/authentication) page for step-by-step SSO configuration instructions.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I stay logged in across sessions?" default-open="false">
    Yes, sessions persist for 30 days unless your organization has SSO timeout policies that override this default.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
